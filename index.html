Battery-care notes

1. Daily driving: a charge limit around 80% is a practical starting point.

2. Need more range: raise the limit for the trip rather than keeping it high every day.

3. Avoid leaving the car at very high or very low state of charge for long periods when practical.

4. Temperature matters. Very hot or cold conditions can temporarily affect displayed range/energy estimates.

5. Watch the trend, not one reading. The tracker uses your measurements to estimate the long-term curve.
